Carse Birch keeps one shared list for your whole team, so everyone sees the same tasks in the same order, the moment they change.

How Carse Birch works

  1. Add a task and it lands at the top of the shared list.
  2. Teammates see it appear without refreshing the page.
  3. Tick it off and it stays on the list, marked done, for the week.

Lists keep the five newest tasks in view, and older ones are archived after seven days.
